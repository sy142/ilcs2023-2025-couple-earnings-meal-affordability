# Figure captions (draft, American English)

Common conventions: husband-only earner couples, squares (brown); dual-earner couples, circles (teal); wife-only earner couples, diamonds (purple). Point estimates are posterior medians; thick lines, 80% and thin lines, 95% credible intervals; nested bands, 50%, 80% and 95% credible bands. Open markers mark comparisons that rely on the share of 0.9 (†). Shaded vertical or horizontal bands, ±2 percentage points; dashed gray lines, zero or the main-model estimate.

**Figure 1.** Study design and analytic sample. (**a**) Flow of household-years from the 2023–2025 cross-sections of the Income and Living Conditions Survey to the common-support sample (Q); band heights are proportional to counts (the wife-only earner band is drawn at a minimum visible height). Exclusions are listed in the order applied (by year in Table S2). The inset shows the 11,192 linkage chains in Q by number of household-year records. Earner-structure percentages are weighted. (**b**) Earner structure and the wife's share of the couple's net labor earnings; the share is defined only for dual-earner couples, and single-earner couples are separate categories.

**Figure 2.** Standardized probability of food deprivation by earner structure and the wife's share of earnings (main model, M2). (**a**) Dual-earner couples: posterior median (line) with nested 50%, 80% and 95% pointwise credible bands for shares 0.05–0.76 (within the weighted 2.5th–97.5th percentiles of observed shares, 0.04–0.77); open circle, posterior median with 80% and 95% credible intervals at a share of 0.9. Square and diamond, husband-only and wife-only earner couples. The shaded area at the bottom shows the weighted distribution of shares. All probabilities are standardized to the covariates of the common-support sample. (**b**) Prespecified comparisons in percentage points; shaded band, ±2 points. † Relies on the share of 0.9, which is supported by few household-years.

**Figure 3.** Wife-only earner couples by the husband's status, and sensitivity of the comparisons. (**a**) Short vertical marks, crude weighted prevalence of food deprivation; diamonds and lines, $E_{3a,k} = p_{F,k} - p_D(0.9)$ (posterior median with 80% and 95% credible intervals), with both probabilities standardized to the couples in each status; shaded band, ±2 points. Comparisons not relying on the share of 0.9 are given in Table S17. (**b**) Comparisons in the main model (first row) and in sample, estimation and target-population variants. Circles, posterior medians with 80% (thick) and 95% (thin) credible intervals; open diamond and dashed line, survey-weighted estimate and 95% confidence interval; shaded bands, ±2 points; dashed gray lines, main-model estimates. Panels have different horizontal scales. † As in Figure 2.

**Figure S1.** Overlap of the estimated probabilities of the five comparison categories used for the common support. **Figure S2.** Prior predictive checks and the linkage-chain variance. (**a**) Weighted prevalence of food deprivation and (**b**) the wife-only versus husband-only difference ($E_4$) simulated from the priors. (**c**) Standard deviation of linkage-chain intercepts: half-normal (HN) prior with scale 1 (scaled ×3 for visibility) and posterior distributions under HN priors with scale 1 (main model), 2 and 5.

**Figure S3.** Posterior predictive checks of the main model. Observed weighted prevalence (dark circles) and 95% posterior predictive intervals (bars, in the tone of the earner category; light to dark teal for low to high shares) by (**a**) earner structure and survey year, (**b**) earner structure and wife's share and (**c**) NUTS-2 region. (**d**) Share of two-record linkage chains with the same outcome in both records: observed (line) and replicated with newly drawn chain intercepts under the main model, alternative half-normal (HN) priors for the chain SD and a model without chain intercepts.
